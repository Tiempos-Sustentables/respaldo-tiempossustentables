The site primarily features infographics, data visualizations and event‑related graphics that make ESG and sustainability information easier to understand for decision‑makers. The visual language favors a consistent palette of greens and deep blues combined with light neutrals, reinforcing both the environmental focus and the editorial, data‑driven nature of the brand. Images should highlight Latin American geographies, industrial and urban landscapes, corporate contexts and concrete sustainability initiatives, as well as conferences and expert exchanges, always prioritizing transparency, factual accuracy and regional relevance over generic stock imagery.
